FAQ

Good to know.

Where is Flamebachi located?

Downtown Los Angeles at 225 W 7th St, Los Angeles, CA 90014, and Jurupa Valley at 10255 Mission Blvd, Jurupa Valley, CA 92509.

How late is Flamebachi open?

Downtown LA is open until 11 PM Monday to Thursday, until 2 AM on Friday and Saturday, and until 10 PM on Sunday. Jurupa Valley is open until 9:45 PM Monday to Thursday, 10:45 PM Friday and Saturday, and 10 PM Sunday.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. Order online and choose your location. Our Jurupa Valley location is pickup and delivery.

What's on the menu?

Hibachi plates with NY steak, chicken, shrimp, salmon and lobster, bachi bowls, garlic noodles, burritos and agua frescas. See the full menu on our menu page.

Is there a lunch deal?

Every day until 4 PM: 10% off all bachi bowls plus a free soda, or 50% off any agua fresca.
